# Library (SAMPLE — replace with your college's real notice)

The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days and
from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM on Saturdays. It remains closed on Sundays and
public holidays.

Every student can issue up to 3 books at a time for 14 days. Books can
be renewed once online if no other student has reserved them. A late
return fine of 5 rupees per day per book is charged after the due date.

The reading hall on the first floor is open during library hours and
provides seating for 200 students. Silence must be maintained inside
the reading hall at all times.
