# Scholarships (SAMPLE — replace with your college's real notice)

Government scholarships are available for students from reserved
categories and for students with a family income below the prescribed
limit. Applications are submitted online through the state scholarship
portal every academic year.

To apply for a scholarship, students need their income certificate,
caste certificate (if applicable), previous year marksheet, bank
passbook copy, and Aadhaar card. The scholarship cell in the
administrative block verifies documents before forwarding them.

Scholarship renewal requires a minimum attendance of 75 percent and
passing all subjects in the previous year. Students who fail a subject
must reapply after clearing it.
