# Fees (SAMPLE — replace with your college's real notice)

Semester fees must be paid online through the student portal before
the deadline announced each semester. A late fee of 500 rupees is
charged for payments made after the deadline, and 1000 rupees after an
additional 15 days.

Students facing genuine financial difficulty may apply for fee
installments by submitting a written request to the principal's office
before the deadline. Installments are granted in two parts: 60 percent
with the first deadline and 40 percent within one month.

Fee receipts are generated instantly on the portal after payment.
Students should keep the receipt safe, as it is required for exam form
submission and hall ticket issuance.
