# Examinations (SAMPLE — replace with your college's real notice)

University examinations are held at the end of each semester. Students
must fill the exam form online before the deadline and pay the exam fee
to receive their hall ticket.

Hall tickets are issued from the student portal one week before the
exams begin. Students must carry the hall ticket and their identity card
to every exam, and reach the exam hall at least 30 minutes early.

Re-evaluation (rechecking) applications open within 15 days of the
result declaration. The re-evaluation fee is non-refundable, and the
revised result is final.
