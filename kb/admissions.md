# Admissions (SAMPLE — replace with your college's real notice)

Admission to the first year is done through the central admission
committee. Applicants must submit their 12th standard marksheet, school
leaving certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and passport-size
photographs at the time of reporting.

The admission helpdesk works on all working days from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
in the administrative block. Seat allotment follows the merit list
published by the committee, and vacant seats are filled in later rounds.

Students must confirm admission by paying the first semester fee within
the deadline mentioned in the allotment letter, otherwise the seat is
offered to the next candidate on the waiting list.
