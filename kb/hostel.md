# Hostel (SAMPLE — replace with your college's real notice)

The college hostel provides shared rooms with mess facilities. Hostel
admission is allotted on merit and distance from the student's hometown,
and preference is given to first-year students from outside the city.

Hostel entry timing is 10:30 PM. Students must sign the entry register
on return. Leave applications must be approved by the warden in advance
for any night stay outside the hostel.

The hostel mess serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Mess charges are
collected monthly along with the hostel fee, and the menu is displayed
on the hostel notice board every week.
